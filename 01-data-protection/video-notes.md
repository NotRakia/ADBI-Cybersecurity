## 2. Cybersecurity

### 2.1 Definition of Cybersecurity

- Cybersecurity is discussed as a way that data, systems, devices, and networks are protected against disclosure, theft of or damage to hardware, software, or electronic data, also the disruption or misdirection of services that these systems offer. 
- Cybersecurity is also known as computer security or information technology security. 
- Cybersecurity includes techniques combined with people along with the processes and are designed to protect digital infrastructures and sensitive data in order to secure the continuity of business and avoid financial losses.

### 2. Characteristics of Cybersecurity

- A topic that nobody thinks of unless bad thing happen
- A topic that is not given much attention in business planning
- A topic that is too technical for everyday users to deal with
- A topic that we cannot afford to ignore and hope for the best
- With how many different technologies exist especially interned connected appliances, everyone and every organization are in many ways exposed to vulnerabilities
- It is like a constantly changing or evolving environment like in health where there are many types of bacteria and viruses affecting our health. Cybersecurity is like in our health is like a hygiene in order to have precautionary measures against contaminants.

### 2.1 Importance of Cybersecurity

Security is no longer a requirement of IT or Security Administrators, it is a priority for all individuals, businesses, and other entities that are connected with any kind of data.

## 3. Cyberattacks

### 3.1 Historical Background

The first data hacking device was recorded back in 1834 wherein transmissions were done by positioning movable arms on top of the towers to show letters and numbers which were then observed from another tower via telescope.

### 3.2 Cyberattack Capabilities and Data Security

Modern or recent cyberattacks can steal credit card details instantaneously without being noticed from distance.

Data security issues are inherent part of data transmission and it changes along with technology no matter what the method or time hacking takes.

Cybersecurity is not a fixed science it constantly evolves to stay ahead of existing threats.

With the development of technology keeping data secure becomes more challenging partly due to growing range and complexity of smart devices, as well partly because of those who are skilled enough to abuse these systems.

There are billions of devices connected to the internet globally, and all those devices contains sensitive data, this makes cybersecurity existential as closing your door when you leave your house or the office.

Cybersecurity is all about managing and mitigating risks. Well managed risk builds trust between businesses, customers, and authorities by ensuring each other that data is well protected.

### 5.1 Undetected Cyberattacks and Business Risk

Vast number of cyberattacks are undetected, big cyberattacks make it to the news and media, but smaller ones are unrecognized or not recognized in time but still causes significant damage.

Cybersecurity Atacks are considered by many as business risk, but they often find out their own data breaches from others such as customers, business partners, or even the police and they do not have a formal cybersecurity incident response plan.

## 6. Key Takeaways
- Cyberattacks are mostly undetected.
- There is no one who is entirely safe against Cybercrime even big companies.
- Issues are always present and it evolves along with technology.
- We should be vigilant and be aware of Cybersecurity, it should be top of mind for us not just IT personnel.
- Cybesecurity builds trust since it gives an assurance for business partners, customers, etc. that data is protected.
- Cybercrime causes the costliest damage and can happen regardless of where you are.

## 7. Practical Application
